    Add your version

    Share your own sheet music or chords for this song. Everyone who opens the song can choose it instead of the automatic version, and say whether it is accurate.

    MusicXML from MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale or Dorico (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl), a chord sheet (.txt, ChordPro) or a ChordListener file (.json).

    With bar lines (| C | G |) each bar is one bar of the song. Without them, the chords are lined up with what ChordListener hears; check them afterwards.

    Paste lyrics

    One line of the song per row. Each line is spread over the bars you choose; fine-tune afterwards under each bar.
