guitar.onnx — high-resolution guitar transcription model

Model weights: guitar-gaps.pth from https://huggingface.co/xavriley/midi-transcription-models
by Xavier Riley (Centre for Digital Music, Queen Mary University of London),
released under the MIT licence (text below). It adapts the high-resolution
piano transcription model of Kong et al. (ByteDance) to the guitar, trained on
the GAPS classical guitar dataset:

  X. Riley, Z. Guo, D. Edwards, S. Dixon. "GAPS: A Large and Diverse Classical
  Guitar Dataset and Benchmark Transcription Model." ISMIR 2024.
  X. Riley, D. Edwards, S. Dixon. "High Resolution Guitar Transcription via
  Domain Adaptation." ICASSP 2024.

Changes made: fine-tuned on GuitarSet players 00-02 (steel-string acoustic
guitar; Q. Xi, R. Bittner, J. Pauwels, X. Ye, J. P. Bello, "GuitarSet: A
Dataset for Guitar Transcription", ISMIR 2018, https://zenodo.org/records/3371780,
CC BY 4.0) with tools/guitar_finetune.py; then converted from PyTorch to ONNX
with the spectrogram front end moved outside the graph, and its weights stored
as 16-bit floats (tools/transcriber_export.py).
