piano.onnx — high-resolution piano transcription model

Model weights: "High-resolution Piano Transcription with Pedals by Regressing
Onsets and Offsets Times", checkpoint CRNN_note_F1=0.9677_pedal_F1=0.9186.pth,
by Qiuqiang Kong, Bochen Li, Xuchen Song, Yuan Wan and Yuxuan Wang (ByteDance),
published at https://zenodo.org/records/4034264 under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made: only the note part of the checkpoint is kept (the pedal model is
dropped); the network was converted from PyTorch to ONNX with the spectrogram
front end moved outside the graph, and its weights stored as 16-bit floats
(tools/transcriber_export.py).

The decoding of the model's outputs in chordlistener/highres.py is a
re-implementation of piano_transcription_inference
(https://github.com/qiuqiangkong/piano_transcription_inference), licensed as follows:
